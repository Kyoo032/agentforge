# Mascot

Nultron is the desk's one character, drawn by one component, `NultronMascot` (`apps/web/components/nultron/nultron-mascot.tsx`): a root `chat-mascot` with `data-mascot="nultron"`. What it does is data, 21 states in `apps/web/lib/mascot-states.ts`; what it looks like is a set of pre-rendered 3D images named by `apps/web/components/nultron/images/manifest.json` (a still per state, a one-shot clip for six states, a four-frame loop for thirteen working states). Job desks place it through `MascotSlot`; Chat's hero and live turn are in [chat.md](./chat.md). The map is [`docs/internal/maps/mascot-and-brand.md`](../../../../docs/internal/maps/mascot-and-brand.md).

## Sub-features

- `mascot-states` — `data-state` is one of the 21 `MASCOT_STATES`: wave, idle, sleep, thinking, writing, answering, searching, calculating, charting, reviewing, listening, painting, filming, editing, presenting, celebrating, error, surprised, love, charging, lets-go. The accessible name and tooltip come from `common.mascot.<state>` (en and id), unless the mascot is `decorative` (`aria-hidden`, no role, name or tooltip).
- `mascot-picture` — inside the root, `.nx-art` holds `img.nx-still` (the still: `full/<state>` at 64 px and up, `head/<state>` under 64 px), and on the body only a `.nx-strip` (a `once` state with a clip) or a `.nx-loop` (a working state with a loop, **mounted only while `data-busy="true"`**: an idle body has no `.nx-loop` in the DOM and never requests its file). Every mounted image `complete` with `naturalWidth` above 0. `data-clip="true"` and `data-loop="true"` on the root say which the state has. The art carries `data-sheet="ready"` once its strip is loaded and decoded and its still is painted; clip and loop animations run only under it. While a state change is settling, the previous state's art is still in the DOM as `.nx-art.nx-held` (its still only), under the new art.
- `mascot-loading` (2026-09-29) — what a page fetches for the character. Nothing eagerly: `/chat` on a cold load requests 3 images (`full/idle`, `full/wave`, `full/celebrating`, 66 KB), `/market` 4 (`full/wave`, `full/charting`, `full/sleep`, `clips/wave`, 208 KB). The Chat hero's two clips (`clips/wave`, `clips/celebrating`) are requested on the first `pointerenter`, `pointerdown` or focus inside `chat-hero`, before the orb itself is reached; the onboarding hero's `clips/love` on the key step. Stills of the states a mount point can reach are fetched when the browser is idle. No request ever names a strip that no mounted state can play.
- `mascot-motion` — `data-motion` is the performance contract. `still` (idle, sleep): no animation. `once` (wave, celebrating, error, surprised, love, lets-go): one clip when the state is entered, `frames / fps` long (0.83 to 1.17 s), stepped through its frames, the still hidden meanwhile and back at the end. `loop-busy` (the twelve work states and charging): loops only while `data-busy="true"`, stepped with `steps(4)` at the fps the manifest records for that loop (3, 4 or 6). Without `data-busy` nothing under a mascot has an `Infinity` iteration count.
- `mascot-busy` — `data-busy="true"` appears only when the caller says a job is running (`busy`) and `isMascotBusy` agrees: `data-placement="beside"` and a `loop-busy` state. A busy mascot keeps moving while the desk is idle or blurred and stops when the window is hidden. A body (64 px and up) swaps its still for the loop strip; a head chip has no strip and takes a small stepped transform on its still (nod, bob or swell by state).
- `mascot-size` — `data-variant="head"` at box sizes under 64 px, `data-variant="full"` from 64 px. Defaults: `beside` 40 px, `empty` 72 px. Onboarding is 96 px. The Chat hero is sized by CSS (`--orb`, its box is in [chat.md](./chat.md), "Hero").
- `mascot-triggers` — the moments that pick a state (`apps/web/lib/mascot-triggers.ts`). The first-run installer panel (`component-setup`): `charging` and busy while it runs, then `celebrating` or `error`. The rail updates panel (`app-updates-panel`): `charging` and busy while the release downloads, `surprised` when it is ready to install, `error` on a failure, nothing while checking or current. The onboarding hero: `wave` on the welcome step, `idle` on the key step, `love` on the examples step. A new chat's first live turn: `lets-go` for 900 ms, then `thinking` / `answering`.
- `mascot-pose` — the home state of a desk when no phase has arrived: chat `idle`, documents `writing`, research `searching`, finance `calculating`, data `calculating`, market `charting`, legal `reviewing`, meeting `listening`, images `painting`, videos `filming`, music `listening`, edit `editing`, presentations `presenting`, education `presenting`, knowledge `searching`.
- `mascot-placement` — `data-placement="empty"` on an unused desk that shows the character: Meeting, Market (with no tickers inside `market-studio-empty`; with tickers above the board while no job runs), Finance (the chooser at `/finance` with no `task`, and `finance-studio-empty`), Edit (the agent panel), Education (the chooser at `/education`), Legal (the new-matter screen) and Knowledge (Sources). Documents, Research, Data, Images, Videos, Music and Presentations show no empty-desk mascot. `data-placement="beside"` sits next to `JobProgressList` (`finance-progress`, `research-progress`, `data-progress`, `market-progress`, `meeting-progress`, `legal-progress`), beside a busy generate or export control, and beside Knowledge sources while one is indexing.
- `mascot-phase` — the active `job.phase` / `job.step` id picks the state: planning, distilling, debate, risk → `thinking`; searching, reading, indexing, resolving → `searching`; drafting, minuting, saving, translating, synthesis → `writing`; computing, profiling, analyzing, quotes → `calculating`; verifying → `reviewing`; analysts, macro → `charting`; extracting, transcribing → `listening`. An unknown id uses the mode's home state. `failed` → `error`. `done` with no error → `celebrating`.
- `mascot-idle-sleep` — an empty desk waves (`data-state="wave"`) for about 1.4 s, settles on the home state, then shows `sleep` after 45 s unused. `?mascot=<state>` forces that state on every `MascotSlot` and skips both timers; `?mascotMode=<mode>` overrides `data-mode` and with it the home state.
- `mascot-reduced-motion` — with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, `.chat-mascot, .chat-mascot *` get `animation: none !important` and `transition: none !important` (in `apps/web/app/globals.css` and in `components/nultron/nultron.css`). The picture stays: assert `data-state` and the displayed still, not that something moves. A busy body (`[data-busy][data-loop]`) also gets its still back (`.nx-still` opacity 1, `.nx-loop` 0), because without the animation the loop strip would show its frame 0, a 320 px copy of the still.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open a desk with an empty-desk character on Default: `/meeting`, `/market`, `/finance` (no `task`), `/edit`, `/education` (no `task`), `/legal` (new matter), `/knowledge` (Sources). The character waves, then settles on that desk's state. `/meeting` draws it once, in the main pane (the recordings list says only "No meetings yet.").
- Start a job on a desk that has a phase list or a busy generate control: a 40 px head appears beside it and follows the phases.
- Open `/chat`: the empty desk's hero shows the whole body; hover it to see a wave, tap it for a cheer. Send in a new chat and the head beside the live answer says "let's go" before it starts thinking.
- First run: the onboarding hero waves, stays calm on the key step, and shows love on the last step. If the document reader has to be fetched, the installer panel on the key step shows the character charging.
- Packaged public app only: the updates control in the rail footer shows the character charging while a release downloads, surprised when it is ready.
- Drive hooks, not user controls: `?mascot=<state>` and `?mascotMode=<mode>`.

## Gotchas

- Visited modes stay mounted (SKILL.md **G3**). After Chat, `getByTestId("chat-mascot").first()` and `document.querySelector('[data-testid="chat-mascot"]')` return the hidden hero first; scope to the visible mascot or to `[data-mode="<mode>"]`.
- `/meeting` has one visible empty-desk mascot at every width (it had two until 2026-09-29, one in the recordings list). The main pane's follows `?mascot=`.
- **Bytes and blank frames need a cold context.** A new Playwright context per measurement; count `Image` requests under `components/nultron/images/` (a dev server also serves each file as a `?url` module, which is a `Script` request, not an image). A `<html>` reload keeps the warm-up's `Image` objects, so measure the first load of each page. With CDP `Network.setCacheDisabled` on, a new `<img>` requests its URL again even if it was warmed; that is expected, and the held art is what keeps the box from going empty meanwhile.
- **`data-busy` from the console no longer shows a loop.** The loop is mounted by React only while the mascot is busy. Use `?mascot=<state>&mascotBusy=1` (see the Busy loops recipe).
- The desk remounts its mascot about 300 ms after load, when the workspace id arrives. An element found before that detaches; a `once` clip restarts on the new element. Wait for a stable element and query again after any wait.
- The Chat hero ignores `?mascot=`; only `MascotSlot` reads it.
- A hidden pane freezes transitions and animations, and the hero's drift eases `translate`. A read right after a change can show the start value. Take a screenshot first, or inject `* { transition: none !important }` for the read.
- A hidden Browser pane also produces almost no animation frames (60 to 100 `requestAnimationFrame` calls a second when it happens to be shown, 1 to 4 over 1.5 s when it is not, and `getAnimations()` then stays at the first frame, so a strip's `translateX` reads 0 and a clip looks stuck). Anything timed (a clip's frame steps, a loop's four offsets, reduced motion, dark or 2x rendering) belongs in headless Chromium through `playwright-core` from the repo's `node_modules`, in a script file under the scratchpad, sampling `getComputedStyle(...).transform` inside a `requestAnimationFrame` loop; it renders real frames and never touches the operator's browser storage. For the state and file checks (the 21 states, the manifest files, sizes, no `Infinity` iteration outside `data-busy`) a same-origin `iframe` inside a Browser tab works and is fast, at 1280 x 800 or 375 x 812 (read `checkVisibility()` and `getBoundingClientRect()` inside the frame; finish the entrance animations first with `document.getAnimations().forEach((a) => a.finish())`, or the boxes read 2 to 4 px small).
- The shared fronted Browser tab is driven by other agents as well: a tab you fronted can navigate under you between two calls. Work in your own background tab and pass its `tabId` every time.
- Dark mode is a class on `<html>`, and the app writes `light` during boot. Swap it after the mascot is stable, and put `light` back; the swap stores nothing in `agentforge-theme`.
- `?mascot=` is a drive hook, not a user control. Do not wait 45 s for `sleep` on a normal drive; use `?mascot=sleep`.
- Setting `data-busy` from the console proves the CSS, not the trigger: it bypasses `isMascotBusy`. Prove the trigger on a stub desk with a real job or send.
