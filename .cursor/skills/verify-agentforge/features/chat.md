# Chat

Chat is the default assistant: model picker, composer, thinking toggle, usage chip, and its own sessions at `/chat`. Since 2026-09-17 the sessions live **in the left rail** under `mode-chat` (`rail-thread-list`: `new-chat-link`, 4 rows, and a `threads-see-all` toggle that expands the list in place). There is no session column any more — `/chat` is just the chat pane. Stub replies without a gateway key; a saved key uses the live Toko Token gateway. A turn is three layers: **Thinking** (collapsible), **tools** (one row per call), **output** (the answer only — never a copy of the prompt, never a `Stub reply` prefix). Maps: [`chat-send.md`](../../../../docs/internal/maps/chat-send.md) and [`chat-sessions-and-rail.md`](../../../../docs/internal/maps/chat-sessions-and-rail.md).

## Sub-features

- `chat-open` shows the empty Chat home with composer, model picker, and the composer toolbar (`composer-toolbar`: `composer-attach` as a 32px icon, `model-picker`, `reasoning-effort`, `composer-enhance`, `composer-send`). The toolbar is one row only when the composer is wide (about 626px and up); narrower it is two rows and then compact chips — see `chat-composer-layout`. Header chips sit in `chat-header`, one row at every width, and fold into one menu when they do not fit (`chat-header-menu`).
- `chat-composer-layout` (2026-09-29) — the composer and the empty-state intent cards size to the room they have (CSS container queries on `composer`), not to the viewport. Four layouts by the composer's content width (its box minus 26px): **wide** ≥ 600 one row, vertically centred; **medium** 470–599 controls on the top row, attach left and `composer-send` right beneath; **narrow** 220–469 compact chips (no visible `Model:` prefix, Thinking as icon plus value, icon-only Enhance, 40px tap targets, model chip on the top row with Thinking, attach and Enhance beside `composer-send` beneath); **tiny** under 220 a wrapping flex. `composer-needs-key` spans the full width in all four. The prompt box is two lines tall below 600, and still grows to 160px. `chat-suggestions` is 1 column when narrow and 2 when its grid is 524px or wider. Map: [`chat-send.md`](../../../../docs/internal/maps/chat-send.md) § 8.
- `chat-header-menu` (2026-09-29) — `chat-header` is one row, 56px tall, at every width. Title left; `chat-context` and `chat-usage` (and `chat-account` when signed in) right **while they fit** beside the title (`data-mode="inline"`). When they do not, they fold into one 40px button, `chat-header-menu` ("Chat details" / "Detail obrolan", a ⋯ icon; `aria-haspopup="dialog"`, `aria-expanded`), and the header is `data-mode="menu"`. The button opens `chat-header-controls`, the panel of the same three chips as full-width rows; Enter, Space or ArrowDown open it and put focus on the first row, ArrowDown / ArrowUp / Home / End and Tab travel the rows, Escape or a press outside closes it, and Escape returns focus to the button. What decides is measured (the title's floor of 120px plus the gap plus the chips' natural width against the header's room, with 12px of hysteresis), not a viewport, and it is re-measured when the chip set changes (the usage chip arrives after the first reply). Each chip is mounted once and never remounted by a fold, so the usage chip's fetch is not repeated. In a menu row a chip's own popover unfolds inside its row instead (`chat-context-breakdown` and the account's `chat-account-signout` render in the row, no portal). Recipe: "Header" below. Map: [`chat-send.md`](../../../../docs/internal/maps/chat-send.md) § 8.
- `chat-hero` (2026-09-29) — the empty desk's hero (`chat-hero`, inside `chat-empty`) sizes to the width of the Chat column, not the viewport: a fluid headline, proportional padding, a status pill that never wraps, shapes that thin out from 2 to 7 as the column widens and never sit on text, and a hero short enough that the first idea card clears the composer at 375 x 812. It reacts cheaply: shapes drift and the mascot's eyes follow a fine pointer, the mascot (`chat-hero-mascot`, a button) waves on hover and cheers with confetti on a tap, a tapped shape pops; all loops pause off screen or in a hidden tab, and reduced motion turns every reaction off. Recipe: "Hero" below. Map: [`chat-send.md`](../../../../docs/internal/maps/chat-send.md) § 9.
- `chat-thinking-chevron` (2026-09-29) — the Thinking select draws no native arrow (`appearance: none`); its pill draws an `aria-hidden` chevron 12px from the pill's outer edge in every composer layout, and a click on it opens the select. Recipe: "Thinking chevron" below.
- `chat-gutter` (2026-09-29) — `chat-header`, the error row, `message-list` and `composer` share one side gutter, `--chat-gutter`: 24px, 12px at a 639px viewport and under, so their left and right edges line up on a phone. Chat only; job-mode headers are not part of it. Recipe: "Gutters" below.
- `chat-empty-state` (0.15.0) is three things inside `chat-empty`: the headline, `chat-key-status` (one status line, `chat-empty-settings` while the status is not connected, and `chat-whats-this` → `chat-whats-this-body`), and `chat-suggestions` — **four** `chat-suggestion` intent cards with `data-idea` `plan` / `summarise` / `rewrite` / `decide`. A card **fills** `composer-text` with its prompt; it never sends. There is no checklist, no mode cards and no model strip. `chat-key-status` follows the gate, not the key, and until the first `GET /api/v1/settings` has answered its pill is `invisible` and `aria-hidden` with no Settings button (sample it across a reload: the first frame must never read `Connect a key to go live` on a connected desk): `status: "ok"` → `Model key connected` and no Settings link; `stub` / `needs_key` → `Connect a key to go live`. `chat-empty-settings` is `btn-primary` only when `gateway.allowed` is false. On stub (`allowed: true`) it is a plain button, `composer-send` stays enabled, and `composer-needs-key` has count 0. "Offline demo" was removed in 0.15.0. The empty desk also shows `chat-mascot` `data-state="idle"`: the whole Nultron character in the hero (`data-variant="full"`, `data-motion="still"`).
- `chat-mascot` — the Nultron character, `NultronMascot` (`data-mascot="nultron"`); states, sizes, clips and loops are [mascot.md](./mascot.md). On the empty desk the hero shows the whole body (`data-variant="full"`, decorative: the button carries the name) in `chat-hero-mascot`: `idle` at rest, `wave` while the pointer or focus is on it, `celebrating` for 1.4s after a tap. Its box is square, 60 to 68px at window heights of 860 or less (and at 901 to 960), 72 to 96px from 961 to 1040, 160px above. Beside a live turn it is a 40px head (`data-variant="head"`, decorative): `lets-go` for the first 900ms of a new chat's first turn, then `thinking` while there is no answer text, `answering` once `message-output` streams, and `error` when the send failed; `data-busy="true"` only in `thinking` and `answering` while the run is live. A pane that could not open still puts the 40px `error` mascot beside `chat-error`. A refused send shows its sentence on `composer-error` only. The same component is placed on the other job desks through `MascotSlot` (`empty` on the unused desk, `beside` a running job). States are data in `lib/mascot-states.ts`. The picture is a pre-rendered 3D image per state (`components/nultron/images/`).
- `chat-embeds` — a paragraph that is only a link, a file URL, an image, or a job URL renders `message-embed` (`data-kind` `link` / `file` / `image` / `job` / `video`). A paragraph that is only `![alt](https://…)` is `data-kind="image"` with that href and no `<img>` — the remote picture is not fetched. The same syntax for `/api/v1/media/…` or a raster `data:image/…` URL still has an `<img>`. A plain `[shot.png](url)` stays `data-kind="file"`. A text-file attachment inlined as its own `--- name ---` line renders as a file card. A link or image syntax inside a sentence stays an ordinary anchor. Search hits and a titled tool payload render as `job` cards under the answer; a calculator number does not.
- `chat-model-switch` — clicking `model-picker` opens `model-picker-panel`, a **portalled** (`document.body`, `fixed`) list placed above the composer. Every option must be clickable with the mouse, not only reachable by Ctrl/Cmd+K and arrows. Clicking one changes the trigger label, survives a reload (`agentforge-chat-model`), and the next send posts that id in the run body. The trigger must stay readable on a narrow pane: outside the narrow layout `model-picker` keeps its 144px floor (`.model-picker`, 288px cap) and the controls wrap; inside it (composer content width under 470) the floor is released and the chip takes the top row's free width (131px at a 375px phone), showing the name without the `Model:` prefix and ending in an ellipsis when it must truncate. `composer-send` stays visible and clickable inside `composer` in every layout.
- `chat-usage` shows the Chat header chip (`chat-usage`), a link to `/usage`. It has three labels, all prefixed `Usage ·` (`Pemakaian ·` on `id`): `Usage · —` while loading and for **every** unusable state (no wallet payload, `needs_key`, `error`, fetch failure), `Usage · Unlimited`, or `Usage · <left>` with the remaining USD. There is no `No key saved` and no `Usage unavailable` copy here — those belong to `settings-gateway-status`. On an `error` wallet the reason is only in the `title` attribute (`apps/web/components/chat-usage-chip.tsx:41-66`).
- `chat-context` shows the Chat header context chip (`chat-context`): a ring plus a short label (`<N> left` or `<N> used`). Click opens `chat-context-breakdown` with Conversation, Attachments, Knowledge (Soul / Memories / Sources, including RAG `N chunks · rag` or `fts` when retrieve ran), and Free. The `used / window` (empty: `0 / window`) line lives **inside the breakdown**, not on the closed chip. Sources excludes the thread's own work card (`threadId` on the context call).
- `chat-ingest` — every completed assistant turn rewrites one `Chat` work card per thread in the Knowledge Base (latest user + assistant, capped). `/knowledge` shows a `Chat` row named after the thread. Retrieval for that thread skips it. See [knowledge-ingest.md](./knowledge-ingest.md).
- `chat-enhance` rewrites the composer draft via `composer-enhance` (`POST /api/v1/prompts/enhance`). Stub rewrites locally. `composer-enhance-revert` / `aria-pressed` restores the pre-enhance text. A second sparkle after an edit treats the box as a new seed. Cancel aborts and does not replace the box.
- `chat-thinking` shows `reasoning-effort` next to the model picker, labeled **Thinking** (`Off` / `Light` / `Normal` / `Deep` / `Extra` / `Max` / `Ultra`). Values stay `none` / `low` / `medium` / `high` / `xhigh` / `max` / `ultra`. Default `medium` (Normal). Switching models does **not** yank the picker — the host snaps silently per model+wire. `Off` skips thinking events unless the model forbids `none` (GPT-6 → Light on the wire). Reasoning models also carry a `model-thinking-badge` in the picker. In the narrow layout the chip shows a bulb icon plus the value, and the word `Thinking` is visually hidden but still the select's accessible name. The select's own arrow is switched off and the pill draws a chevron instead (`chat-thinking-chevron`). There is **no** `chat-wire` control.
- `chat-send` puts the user prompt in the transcript and returns the send button to `Send`. **Enter** sends (`submitOnEnter`); Shift+Enter stays newline. Packaged window must do this, not only `:3000`.
- `chat-keep-alive` — switching Chat ↔ Documents (or any rail job mode) must not unmount the visited mode. Drafts and in-flight runs survive. `WorkModeKeepAlive` keeps visited modes mounted.
- `chat-fail-closed` — a gateway 400/403 (illegal `temperature`, no access, quota) surfaces on `composer-error` only. `chat-error` stays clear. Running/Thinking must clear (`onFailed` sets `running` false and `sendFailed` so the live mascot is `error`). Do not hang.
- `chat-probe` — host still retries contacting the model up to 3 times internally. The UI stays `Thinking…` (`thinking-placeholder` inside `message-thinking`) and `Sending…` on `composer-send`. Probe copy (`Probing`, `1st try`) must not appear on the frontend. After 3 failed tries, `composer-error` reads `Could not reach {model} after 3 tries` and `chat-error` stays clear. Packaged window must do this, not only `:3000`.
- `chat-new` starts a blank session from rail `new-chat-link` (including when the rail is collapsed) without losing the previous thread in the list. The model picker keeps its current value (does not snap to the catalog default). There is no header `new-chat`.
- `chat-switch` reopens the first thread from the rail block (`rail-thread-list` → `thread-item`). The picker restores that thread's last model from localStorage. If the destination thread has no stored model (or the stored id left the catalog), the picker keeps its current value — never the catalog default. First Chat load uses last-used (`agentforge-chat-model`), then catalog default.
- `chat-rail` keeps `mode-chat` visible. On Default, Documents/Research/Images/Videos/Presentation are also visible. `mode-agents` count is 0.
- `chat-rail-sessions` — directly under `mode-chat` the expanded rail shows `rail-thread-list`: `new-chat-link` (`New chat`), then at most `RAIL_RECENT_THREADS` (4) newest `thread-item` rows each with a hover `thread-delete`, then `threads-see-all` **only** when more than 4 exist. `rail.groupJobs` (`Make` / `Buat`) sits below all of it. A collapsed rail keeps `new-chat-link` and hides the session rows. A failed `GET /api/v1/threads` or a refused `DELETE` renders `rail-thread-error` inside `rail-thread-list`, under `new-chat-link`; the previously loaded rows stay on screen. A blank rail is a different bug from an error line.
- `chat-threads-expand` — `threads-see-all` is a toggle button, not a link: pressed (`aria-expanded="true"`) it lists every thread the host returned (capped at 40) in the same `rail-thread-list` and reads `Show less`; pressed again it collapses back to 4. The state is component-local — a reload starts collapsed. There is **no** second column and no route that opens one.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open `http://127.0.0.1:3000/chat`.
- Choose `Chat` on the left rail (`mode-chat`).
- `/` redirects to the first visible mode (Chat on Default).
- `/agents/<uuid>` redirects to Chat (Build is parked).

Preconditions:

- Doctor exits 0 against `http://127.0.0.1:3000`.
- You are proving Chat at `/chat`.
- Unique prompt text, e.g. `VERIFY chat <run-id>: What is 2 + 3?`.

## Gotchas

- The composer toolbar is a container-query grid (`.composer-toolbar`, `apps/web/app/globals.css`), and its controls group (`.composer-controls`) **wraps** (`flex-wrap: wrap`); that group must never go back to `overflow-hidden`. It used to clip, which hid the model palette (a thin sliver in the 32px toolbar row, clicks falling through to `message-list`) and squeezed the trigger to 18px on narrow panes. Any popover anchored in that group (model picker, and anything added next to it) **must** be portalled to the body and positioned with `placePickerPanel` (`apps/web/lib/picker-panel.ts`). Playwright still calls a clipped option "visible", so assert the click actually changes the trigger label — a visibility check alone will not catch it.
- **The whole desk pauses its loops when the window is not focused, and the Browser pane is not focused between calls.** `<html data-ambient="paused" data-ambient-reason="blurred">` and 0 running loops on a fresh read is the switch working, the same way `data-paused` below is. `left_click` the page before counting running animations; a click is input, so an idle desk wakes too. Do not remove the attribute in code to "fix" a count you will rely on later. Entrances are never paused by it.
- **The hero pauses its loops in a hidden tab, and the Browser pane is hidden between calls.** A read of `chat-hero` `data-paused` after a tool call says `"true"`, its transitions are frozen mid-flight and `getAnimations()` shows 0 running: that is the pause working, not a stuck hero. Do not "fix" it by unpausing in code. Force `document.hidden` false (see "Hero") or read in the same batch as a screenshot. A screenshot right after a DOM change can also show the previous frame; take it again. A viewport under 768px emulates touch, so drive the pointer effects at the pane's own size or 1280px; and a screenshot of an emulated viewport larger than the pane is scaled down, so measure and use the picture only to look.
- **Composer widths are content widths, and thresholds can be off by a pixel.** The queries read the composer's content box (border box minus 26px), so a 626px composer is the first wide one, not a 600px one. In a scaled or zoomed browser pane the 1px border floors to a device pixel, which moves a boundary by under a pixel; drive a width one or two px either side of a threshold, not on it. The `mobile` viewport preset also swaps the user agent and turns on touch; reset it with `preset desktop` as soon as the check is done, the pane may keep it otherwise.
- In the medium and narrow layouts `composer-attach` is first in the DOM and second on screen, so Tab goes attach → model → Thinking → Enhance → Send while the eye goes model → Thinking → attach → Enhance → Send. That is a known focus-order mismatch of the two-row layouts (wide matches). Do not "fix" it by reordering the DOM without checking wide.
- The second column is gone for good (deleted 2026-09-17), so a 480px window leaves the chat pane ~248px (rail 232 only) whatever the session list is doing — expanding `threads-see-all` only makes the rail scroll, never narrower panes. It used to be ~48px because rail 232 + thread list 199 were both fixed with no responsive auto-collapse. Collapse the rail (`rail-collapse`) to judge the pane below 480px.
