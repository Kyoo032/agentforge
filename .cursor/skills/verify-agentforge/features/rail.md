# Rail

The rail is the left column every screen shares: the product mark and desk switcher on top, then three labelled groups — Converse (Chat plus the session list), Make (one tab per `productMode` the current desk owns; the English label is `Make`, Bahasa Indonesia is `Buat`), Account (Knowledge Base, Channels, Workspaces, Usage, Settings) — and a footer that is one aligned row of 32px icon squares. Theme and collapse are always there. The updates icon is packaged public DPSBuddy only; webdev and the hosted web render none. Labels on the rows stay visible down to 480px; at 480px or narrower the rail starts collapsed. Footer words live in `aria-label` / `title` only. It renders two chrome states from one component: expanded (default 232px, resizable 168–360) and collapsed (a fixed 68px icon column). What it shows is not configuration — it is the current workspace's `productModes` run through `resolveWorkspaceModes`, so a desk that hides a mode also makes its URL unreachable. The bullet names below (`rail-groups`, `rail-modes`, `rail-sessions`, …) are sub-feature names for this file, not testids; the testids are the ones quoted inside each bullet.

## Sub-features

- `rail-groups` is header, Converse (`rail.groupConverse` = `Converse` / `Percakapan`), Make (`rail.groupJobs` = `Make` / `Buat`, omitted entirely when the desk has no non-chat mode), Account (`rail.groupAccount`). Group text exists only when expanded — collapsed renders a 1px divider instead.
- `rail-modes` is one `mode-${href.slice(1)}` per visible mode, in catalog order (`mode-chat`, `mode-documents`, `mode-research`, `mode-finance`, `mode-data`, `mode-market`, `mode-legal`, `mode-meeting`, `mode-images`, `mode-videos`, `mode-music`, `mode-edit`, `mode-presentations`, `mode-education`). Default shows all **fourteen**. `mode-agents` and `mode-channels` count is always 0. No row carries a `New` badge — 0.15.0 deleted the mechanism (`NEW_BADGE_UNTIL`, `newBadgeOn()`, `rail.badgeNew`), so `mode-music` reads exactly `Music` and `mode-meeting` exactly `Meeting`. The current row is marked by its fill and `aria-current="page"` only; the left accent stripe is gone (`border-left-width` 0, no `::before`).
- `rail-finance-tasks` is the Finance sub-list: the Finance row is wrapped in `rail-finance-mode` and carries the chevron `finance-tasks-toggle`; on `/finance` the chevron can open `rail-finance-tasks` with five `finance-task-<id>` rows (closed until pressed) (Financial brief, Cash flow & runway, Budget vs actual, Investment appraisal, Ratio health check). Off `/finance` the chevron is `disabled` and `aria-expanded="false"`. Rows and task behaviour belong to [finance.md](./finance.md).
- `rail-submenu-chevron` — both chevrons (`finance-tasks-toggle`, `rail-market-specialists-toggle`) show a short visible word (`tasks` / `specialists`, `tugas` / `spesialis` in `id`) and keep the full `Show tasks` / `Show specialists` phrase in `aria-label` and `title` (`apps/web/components/rail-submenu.tsx`). The sub-list stays closed until that chevron is pressed, including on the mode's own route. A mode row that reads less than its full name is still the regression to avoid — the short word sits on the chevron, not in place of the mode name.
- `rail-market-specialists` is the Market sub-list's **chrome**: on an expanded rail the Market row is wrapped in `rail-market-mode` and carries `rail-market-specialists-toggle` (the chevron, `aria-expanded`); under it `rail-market-specialists-branch` is the indented wrapper with the hairline guide and `rail-market-specialists` is the `role="group"` scroll container holding one `rail-market-specialist-<id>` row per agent. The block belongs to `/market`: it starts closed even there, the chevron opens it while you stay on the route, and off `/market` the rows are gone and the chevron is `disabled` — nothing about that state is stored, so there is no rail-prefs key to assert. The rows are height-adaptive and carry `data-overflowing` while they are cut off (four at 720 and 768, about seven at 900, all eleven from 1008 up); every rail row is `shrink-0`, so the nav scrolls rather than squashing rows to fit the block in. Which desk a row opens, what it swaps, and every `market-*` testid belong to [market.md](./market.md); this file owns only "the block is there, and it disappears when the rail collapses".
- `rail-sessions` is the CONVERSE session block's **chrome**: expanded, `rail-thread-list` wraps `new-chat-link`, holds up to four `thread-item` rows (`RAIL_RECENT_THREADS = 4`, `apps/web/lib/thread-groups.ts:2`), and shows `threads-see-all` when the host returned more than four and `rail-thread-error` when the list load or a delete failed. Collapsed, the session rows unmount and `new-chat-link` stays as an icon plus `aria-label`. This is the product's only session list. What a row does — open, delete, the confirm copy, the fork race — belongs to [chat.md](./chat.md).
- `rail-account` is the five fixed links no desk can hide: `mode-knowledge` (`/knowledge`), `channels-link` (`/channels`), `workspaces-link` (`/workspaces`), `usage-link` (`/usage`), `settings-link` (`/settings`). All five carry the same testid in both chrome states.
- `rail-brand` is `product-logo` (both states) plus `product-brand` (**expanded only**), which links to the desk's first visible mode. Packaged flavors must not read DPSBuddy — see [desktop-brands.md](./desktop-brands.md).
- `rail-collapse` / `rail-expand` is one button with a flipping testid: `rail-collapse` while expanded, `rail-expand` while collapsed (`apps/web/components/app-rail.tsx`). It writes `agentforge-rail-collapsed` (`"1"` / `"0"`) and sets `data-rail="min"` / `"full"` on the `<aside>`. A viewport at `max-width: 480px` starts collapsed and does not write that key when the button is pressed.
- `rail-resize` is the `role="separator"` drag handle on the right edge, expanded only. Pointer drag sets the width; ArrowLeft/ArrowRight move ±8px, Home snaps to 168, End to 360 (`apps/web/lib/panel-width.ts:3`). It writes `agentforge-rail-width` and `aria-valuenow` tracks it.
- `rail-theme` is `theme-toggle` in the footer. **Default with nothing stored is light** since 0.15.0 (`resolveTheme(null)` → `"light"`, `apps/web/lib/theme.ts`): `<html>` has class `light` and `:root` carries the light values. A stored `dark` adds class `dark` and removes `light`. `aria-label` and `title` read the target mode (`common.theme.dark` = `Dark` on a light desk, `Light` on a dark one); no word is rendered next to the icon in either rail state (`apps/web/components/theme-toggle.tsx:43-45`). The rail follows the theme (`--rail-*` per theme). Type is DM Sans for the UI and Source Serif 4 for headings, bundled locally.
- `rail-updates` is the footer updates control: wrapper `app-updates`, button `app-updates-toggle`, then `app-updates-badge` / `app-updates-panel` / `app-updates-status` / `app-updates-check` / `app-updates-install` / `app-updates-close`. It renders only when the product name is DPSBuddy **and** `isElectron()` is true (`apps/web/lib/use-app-updates.ts`). On webdev and the hosted web, `app-updates` has count 0 — there is no disabled check button. Packaged public DPSBuddy shows the icon; Kemenkeu and Metranet still render none. See [desktop.md](./desktop.md).
- `rail-redirect` is the other half of `productModes`: entering a hidden mode's URL lands on the desk's first visible mode, and `/agents` / `/studio` do the same. `/chat`, `/settings`, `/workspaces`, `/usage` and `/knowledge` are never redirected.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- The rail is on every screen after onboarding. There is no route for it.
- Webdev: `http://127.0.0.1:3000/chat`. Packaged: the Electron window on Chat.
- Collapse or expand it with the footer button; drag its right edge to resize.
- The desk name under the product name opens the switcher; Workspaces / Usage / Settings sit at the bottom of the mode list.

## Driving it with the DPSBuddy harness

Preconditions:

- Doctor exits 0.
- Read `localStorage["agentforge-rail-width"]` and `["agentforge-rail-collapsed"]` **before** touching either, and restore both at the end — they are the operator's chrome, not test state. A throwaway Playwright profile starts empty and needs no restore.

- **Groups (expanded).** With `rail-collapse` visible, the `mode-*` testids are exactly the desk's modes plus `mode-knowledge`. On Default that is all fourteen work modes. Collect them by prefix; do not match group headings by text — they are localized.
- **Sessions.** On a wide window, `rail-thread-list` count 1 and `new-chat-link` count 1. On a desk with history, `thread-item` > 0 and `threads-see-all` count 1; on a fresh desk both are 0 and that is the pass. A viewport at 480px or narrower starts collapsed. At 700px the labels stay: `new-chat-link` is still 1 and `thread-item` is 0 until the rail is expanded. Opening, deleting and the send-too-early race are driven in [chat.md](./chat.md), not here.
- **Collapse.** Click `rail-collapse`. Wait on `rail-expand`. Assert `[data-rail="min"]`, `<aside>` width 68, `localStorage["agentforge-rail-collapsed"] === "1"`, and count 0 for `rail-thread-list`, `rail-resize` and `product-brand`. `workspaces-link`, `workspaces-switcher`, `usage-link`, `settings-link` and `product-logo` stay count 1.
- **Expand.** Click `rail-expand`. Wait on `rail-collapse`. `data-rail` is `full`, width is the stored value, the key reads `"0"`.
- **Resize.** Focus `rail-resize`. `End` → width 360, `Home` → 168, three ArrowRights from 232 → 256. Drag it with the pointer and assert `localStorage["agentforge-rail-width"]` matches the new width. Reload and assert the width survives.
- **Theme.** Read `document.documentElement.className` first and `localStorage["agentforge-theme"]`. With nothing stored, class is `light`. Click `theme-toggle`: class becomes `dark`, key reads `dark`, `aria-label` flips to `Light`. Click again: class `light`, key `light`. There is no `data-theme` attribute. Leave the operator's theme as you found it (a fresh Playwright profile has nothing to restore). Driven 2026-09-23.
- **Footer row.** On webdev and the hosted web, `rail-footer` holds `theme-toggle` and `rail-collapse` (or `rail-expand`), each 32×32 with empty `innerText`, and `app-updates` has count 0. On packaged public DPSBuddy it also holds `app-updates-toggle`. Icon colour is the rail text colour (the 0.15.0 fix for dark-on-dark icons: `[data-testid="rail-footer"] .btn-ghost`).
- **Submenus.** On `/chat` both `finance-tasks-toggle` and `rail-market-specialists-toggle` are `disabled`, visible text `tasks` / `specialists` (`tugas` / `spesialis` on `id`), `aria-label` `Show tasks` / `Show specialists`, and `mode-finance` / `mode-market` read their full names. Click `mode-finance`: the chevron enables with `aria-expanded="false"` and `rail-finance-tasks` has count 0 until you press the chevron. Press it: five rows. Click `mode-market`: the finance list closes (leaving the mode forgets the open), the market chevron enables still closed, and pressing it shows eleven rows. Pressing it again closes the block while you stay on `/market`.
- **Hidden modes.** On a Legal desk, `mode-images` count 0; navigating to `/images` lands on `/chat`. Assert the **rail** testid, never `images-studio` — the keep-alive leaves a hidden studio pane mounted (see Gotchas).
- **Account links.** Each of `mode-knowledge`, `workspaces-link`, `usage-link`, `settings-link` navigates to its path from both chrome states.
- **Locale (id).** The groups read `Percakapan` / `Buat` / `Akun`, the `<aside>` `aria-label` is `Mode produk`, `rail-resize` is `Ubah ukuran navigasi`, `rail-collapse` is `Ciutkan navigasi`. Testids are locale-invariant.
- **IDE proof.** Screenshots of both chrome states under `evidence/rail/<run-id>/`, with the desk name visible.

## Gotchas

- `rail-collapse` and `rail-expand` are the same button with a state-swapped testid. Waiting for the other one is how you know the click landed.
- `product-brand` does **not** exist on a collapsed rail; only `product-logo` does, and it lives inside the compact switcher.
- `rail-resize` and `rail-thread-list` do not exist on a collapsed rail. Count 0 is the expected state.
- Collapsed group labels are a 1px divider, not text. Never identify a group by its string — and never by string at all on an `id` desk.
- First paint is always an expanded 232px rail: collapse and width are read in mount effects, not in the state initializer. Wait for a named testid, not a fixed sleep, before screenshotting chrome.
- A hidden mode's studio is still in the DOM — SKILL.md **Harness-wide gotchas** G3. Driven on 2026-09-17: on a Legal desk `/images` redirected to `/chat` and `images-studio` still had count 1 with `isVisible()` false, so the rail testid is the only honest assertion for "this desk cannot reach that mode".
- Rail tabs come from the workspace, not from agents. A custom agent never adds a tab.
- The rail is `overflow-hidden`, so the switcher menu is portaled to `document.body`. Scope menu queries to the document.
- `mode-usage` and `mode-workspaces` do not exist. Usage and Workspaces are ACCOUNT links, not product modes.
