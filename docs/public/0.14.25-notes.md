# DPSBuddy 0.14.25

## Market

- **Watch a list, then read a briefing.** Type tickers from any market (US, IDX, futures, `^VIX`…) and DPSBuddy shows live prices and charts with no API key. When you want a written briefing, add your gateway key and press **Write a briefing**. This is analysis, not investment advice — it will not tell you to buy or sell now.
- Quotes, charts, headlines, and macro levels are fetched on your machine. You can download a DOCX. The default briefing language is Bahasa Indonesia.

## Knowledge Base

- Knowledge stays on this computer. Chat can cite sources. PDF and Word files can be added. The map shows how facts connect. Finished Chat turns and job results still leave a short card in Sources on their own.

## Workspaces

- Add another desk and pick which tabs it shows. The Default desk cannot be deleted.

## Files to download

- **Windows:** `DPSBuddy-Setup-0.14.25.exe`
- **macOS (preview):** `DPSBuddy-0.14.25-mac-arm64.dmg` for Apple silicon (M1 and later), `DPSBuddy-0.14.25-mac-x64.dmg` for Intel

## macOS (preview)

- Open the `.dmg` and drag **DPSBuddy** to Applications.
- The preview build is not signed by Apple, so macOS will say it cannot verify the developer.
  - macOS 15 and later: double-click the app, dismiss the warning, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
  - macOS 14 and earlier: right-click **DPSBuddy.app**, choose **Open**, then **Open** again.
  - If it still refuses, run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/DPSBuddy.app` in Terminal.
- **Keychain.** When macOS asks, choose **Always Allow**.
- **Closing and quitting.** Closing the window keeps DPSBuddy in the Dock; click the Dock icon to come back. Cmd+Q quits.
- **Video editing needs ffmpeg** (`brew install ffmpeg`). Everything else works without it.
- **Updates on macOS are manual.** Download the new `.dmg` from here and drag it over the old app; your settings and workspaces stay in `~/Library/Application Support/DPSBuddy`.
- Please report anything odd on macOS on the issues page, with your macOS version and chip.
