# DPSBuddy 0.14.26

## Start over

- **Sign out of the gateway.** Settings now has a **Start over** card. **Sign out of the gateway** removes your API key from every desk and returns the app to the welcome screen. Your threads, Knowledge Base and workspaces stay exactly where they are — it is a sign-out, not a delete.
- **Reset to a fresh install.** The second button wipes this computer's DPSBuddy data and restarts the app as if it had just been installed. It asks you to type `RESET` first, and until you restart you can still call it off.

## Gateway status

- Settings shows a status row for your API key: whether the key is working, when it was last checked, and why it is not working if it is not. **Re-check** asks the gateway again on the spot.
- Saving a key now verifies it immediately instead of failing later inside a job. If the key checks out and you go offline, DPSBuddy keeps working for a week on the last good check.

## Bahasa Indonesia everywhere

- Every mode now follows the app language. Chat, Presentation, Documents, Research, Finance, Data, Market, Legal, Images, Videos and Edit all write and label in Bahasa Indonesia when the app is set to Indonesian — including the model pickers, the example galleries, error messages, and the names new conversations are given.
- Images and Videos also set the language of text drawn into the picture or the clip.

## Finance

- **Generate from a typed brief.** Paste or type a brief with figures in it and press Generate — DPSBuddy reads the figures out of your brief and fills the rows for you instead of stopping to tell you to add items first. It says how many figures it found.
- **Figures keep their size.** "IDR 18.4B" is now read as 18,400,000,000, so stored items, the DOCX and the NPV / runway numbers are right.
- **Reliable on the default model.** Finance and Documents used to fail partway through on the model they pick by default, while the model was in fact still working. Runs are now given the time a thinking model needs, and jobs ask for a faster answer. A quiet stretch during drafting is the model thinking, not a stall.
- Downloading a Finance brief as Word is its own button on the studio header again.

## Cost estimate in Images and Videos

- Before you generate, each model shows an estimated price from the model provider's published list price — roughly what an image or a second of video costs — so you can compare models before spending. Each picker row ends with a short price like `$0.03/img` or `$0.12/s`, and the estimate line says how a model compares with the cheapest one.
- This is an estimate at provider list price in USD, not a bill: your gateway charges its own rate, which is currently lower. A few models have no published price yet and say so.

## Also in this release

- **Failure messages arrive sooner and in your language.** When the gateway refuses a request, DPSBuddy now says so in the app language with the status code, instead of passing through whatever the upstream service said.
- **Security hardening** across the app and the desktop shell.
- Chat keeps the composer on one row, the rail and the conversation list can be resized, and an available update is easier to spot.

## macOS (preview)

- Open the `.dmg` and drag **DPSBuddy** to Applications.
- The preview build is not signed by Apple, so macOS will say it cannot verify the developer.
  - macOS 15 and later: double-click the app, dismiss the warning, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
  - macOS 14 and earlier: right-click **DPSBuddy.app**, choose **Open**, then **Open** again.
  - If it still refuses, run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/DPSBuddy.app` in Terminal.
- **Keychain.** When macOS asks, choose **Always Allow**.
- **Closing and quitting.** Closing the window keeps DPSBuddy in the Dock; click the Dock icon to come back. Cmd+Q quits.
- **Video editing needs ffmpeg** (`brew install ffmpeg`). Everything else works without it.
- **Updates on macOS are manual.** Download the new `.dmg` from here and drag it over the old app; your settings and workspaces stay in `~/Library/Application Support/DPSBuddy`.
- Please report anything odd on macOS on the issues page, with your macOS version and chip.
