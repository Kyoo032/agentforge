# DPSBuddy 0.15.0

## The desk got easier to read

- **Light is now the normal look**, with white pages and near-black text. Dark is still there and one click away in the rail; your choice is remembered.
- **Text is darker and larger where it matters.** Nothing in the app is gray-on-gray any more — several labels that were hard to read now meet the accessibility contrast standard.
- **The whole desk floats on a soft blue shadow**, with tighter, squarer corners. It reads as one surface instead of a set of grey boxes.
- **New typeface throughout**, bundled inside the app the same as before. DPSBuddy still works with no internet connection.

## Every mode now tells you what you get

- **Each mode opens with one plain sentence about the result**, not a paragraph of instructions. Finance says: *"You get: a written brief with every core metric — margins, burn, runway, breakeven, NPV — computed in code and traced."*
- **The how-to details moved out of the way.** Optional settings — a discount rate, a resolution, a style tag — are folded under **Advanced** until you want them, so a first run is just the box and the button.
- **Fewer repeated instructions.** Text that told you the same thing three times is gone.

## Chat

- **Four ideas to start from** on an empty chat: shape a plan, summarise something, rewrite for a reader, think a decision through. Each one drops a ready-made prompt into the box for you to edit.
- **Tool activity is folded into "Thinking".** What DPSBuddy looked up or calculated is now inside the thinking panel instead of above your answer, and it is written in plain words — *"Past chats · list → none"* rather than a block of code. It opens while DPSBuddy works and folds away when the answer lands; open it any time to see what it did.
- **The composer is simpler.** One instruction instead of three, and the attach control is a small paperclip in the toolbar.
- **The Thinking control is one clean dropdown** showing just the level — Off, Light, Normal, Deep, Extra, Max, Ultra.

## Fixed

- **"Sign out" looks like it works now.** On a machine with a saved key, signing out forgets the key and returns you to the welcome screen asking for one.
- **Three modes had an invisible text box.** In Documents, Research and Presentation, the prompt field had no border — you could type into an apparently empty space. It now has a visible outline, and it lights up blue when you click into it.
- **The left rail no longer hides two modes.** "Show specialists" and "Show tasks" used to sit right on top of the Market and Finance names, pushing them out of view. The arrows are on their own now.
- **The rail footer buttons were dark-on-dark** and hard to see. All three are even, aligned, 32-pixel buttons.
- **"Offline demo" is gone.** Settings used to say it even when no key was saved, which was simply untrue. It now says what is actually going on: *No key — paste one below.*
- **The Menu and language fields in Meeting** no longer run their labels into their dropdowns.
- **The "New" badges** on Music and Meeting are removed.
- **Selected items in the rail are highlighted by colour alone** — the extra blue stripe down the left edge is gone.

## Better on a big screen

- **Wide windows are used properly.** Pages used to stop at a fixed width and leave a large empty margin either side; they now widen with the window. Lines of prose stay at a comfortable reading width, so text does not stretch across a 32-inch monitor.
- **The chat lines up.** The title, your messages and the composer now share the same column instead of drifting apart.

## Also new since 0.14.27

0.15.0 is the first installer since 0.14.27, so it also carries everything built in between.

### Music

- **A new Music mode.** Describe a song in a sentence, or paste your own lyrics with a style and a title, and DPSBuddy makes the track. Each request comes back as two takes, billed once, and both are saved to the library under the form.
- **Draft lyrics** writes a set of lyrics for you to edit before you spend anything on a song. Style, title and **Instrumental** sit under **Advanced**.
- Music runs on your gateway key, like Images and Videos. A song takes a few minutes; leave the page open while it works.

### Meeting

- **A new Meeting mode.** Give it a recording, or paste a transcript, and you get the transcript, minutes (who attended, what was decided, who owes what by when, risks and open questions), and the same minutes in the other language, English or Indonesian.
- **Record from your microphone.** A **Record** button sits beside the file picker, with pause, resume and a running timer. Recordings stop at 25 MB, about two hours, and keep everything up to that point.
- **A failed upload does not lose the recording.** It stays on screen with **Retry upload**, so you can try again.
- **Reading a recording needs ffmpeg**, the same as video editing. Without it, Meeting tells you so, and a pasted transcript still works.
- Replacing a meeting's recording deletes the old file instead of leaving it on disk.

### Channels

- **Post to Telegram from your desk.** The new **Channels** page connects a Telegram bot you create with @BotFather. Add the groups or channels you have put the bot in, post to one, and press **Check for new messages** to pull in the replies.
- The bot token is stored encrypted on this computer and is never shown again.
- DPSBuddy fetches new messages only when you check. It never opens a way for Telegram to reach into your computer. Messages travel through Telegram in the clear, so post only what you would put in a group chat.

### Fixes and safety underneath

- **Video editing:** silence and scene detection no longer fail with an ffmpeg error on clips whose length is not a whole number of seconds. A file Edit cannot really read is refused when you import it, and nothing is left behind. When ffmpeg does fail, the message no longer shows file paths from your computer.
- **Web pages DPSBuddy fetches for you are checked more strictly.** Addresses inside your own network are refused more thoroughly, including web names that point there and pages that redirect there.
- **Keys and tokens are kept out of the app's own logs.**

## Files to download

- **Windows:** `DPSBuddy-Setup-0.15.0.exe`
- **macOS (preview):** `DPSBuddy-0.15.0-mac-arm64.dmg` for Apple silicon (M1 and later), `DPSBuddy-0.15.0-mac-x64.dmg` for Intel

## Windows

- Run `DPSBuddy-Setup-0.15.0.exe` and follow the installer.
- The installer is not code-signed yet. If SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC", choose **More info → Run anyway**.
- Installing over an existing copy replaces the app and keeps your settings, workspaces and Knowledge Base. Already-installed copies are offered this version in the app.

## macOS (preview)

- Open the `.dmg` and drag **DPSBuddy** to Applications.
- The preview build is not signed by Apple, so macOS will say it cannot verify the developer.
  - macOS 15 and later: double-click the app, dismiss the warning, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
  - macOS 14 and earlier: right-click **DPSBuddy.app**, choose **Open**, then **Open** again.
  - If it still refuses, run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/DPSBuddy.app` in Terminal.
- **Keychain.** When macOS asks, choose **Always Allow**.
- **Closing and quitting.** Closing the window keeps DPSBuddy in the Dock; click the Dock icon to come back. Cmd+Q quits.
- **Video editing needs ffmpeg** (`brew install ffmpeg`). Everything else works without it.
- **Updates on macOS are manual.** Download the new `.dmg` from here and drag it over the old app; your settings and workspaces stay in `~/Library/Application Support/DPSBuddy`.
- Please report anything odd on macOS on the issues page, with your macOS version and chip.

## A note on what changed underneath

Apart from the new modes above, 0.15.0 is a look-and-feel release. Nothing was removed from what 0.14.27 could do — Finance's five tasks, the Market analyst team, the rail sessions, local file reading, the first-run setup and the Knowledge Base all work exactly as before. If a familiar control moved, it is almost certainly behind an **Advanced** line or an arrow now.
