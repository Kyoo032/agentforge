# DPSBuddy 0.14.27

## Finance does five kinds of work

- Finance is no longer one button. Under **Finance** in the left rail you now pick the job: a **brief**, **ratios**, a **budget**, **cash flow**, or an **appraisal**. Each one asks for what it needs and nothing else.
- **The numbers are worked out by DPSBuddy, not written by the model.** Totals, ratios, NPV and runway are calculated from the rows you confirmed. The model names the categories and writes the words around them — it never invents an amount.
- A sheet's own totals are no longer added in twice, so a revenue line stays the size you typed it.
- **Open a spreadsheet or a report instead of typing figures.** Upload an Excel file, a PDF, a Word document or a presentation and DPSBuddy reads the tables out of it, shows you the rows, and waits for you to confirm them — the same step as pasting.
- **One export button.** Send the finished work to **Excel**, **PowerPoint** or **Word**, and DPSBuddy remembers the format you used last. Charts now appear on screen above the written brief.

## Your files are read on your computer

- DPSBuddy reads PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, OpenDocument, RTF and EPUB files **on this machine**. Nothing is sent away to be converted.
- **Scans are not sent anywhere either.** A picture-only PDF is refused with a plain message rather than uploaded to a text-recognition service.
- If a file's name and its contents disagree, it is refused instead of guessed at.

## First run sets itself up

- DPSBuddy now installs the pieces it needs by itself the first time you open it. You will see a short setup step during the welcome screens, and nothing to download or install by hand.
- It only ever fetches the exact package it expects, and checks it before using it. If the step cannot finish, the app still opens and still works — file reading simply falls back to the simpler reader.
- Video editing still needs ffmpeg on macOS; that one is next.

## Market Watch

- **The specialists moved into the left rail.** Click **Market** and every specialist is listed underneath — stocks, crypto, forex, gold and minerals, commodities, indices, news, summary, scanner and the rest. Picking one switches the whole desk.
- **Each specialist keeps its own watchlist.** The tickers you add to crypto stay on crypto; stocks keeps its own.
- **A team of analysts, on request.** Instead of a single pass, a briefing can be built by several analysts working on the same packet, followed by a bull case, a bear case, a risk read and a balanced summary. It gives no rating, no price target and no instruction to buy or sell, and it always carries the disclaimer.
- **More free sources**, with no extra key to buy: CoinGecko and funding rates for crypto, metals, company fundamentals and insider activity, global macro headlines, and public discussion from Reddit and StockTwits. Every number in the briefing is still worked out by DPSBuddy.

## Chat sessions in the left rail

- Your recent conversations now sit under **Chat** in the left rail, with **All sessions** opening the full list in place. The separate conversation column is gone, which gives the chat itself the width back.
- Opening an old conversation and typing straight away no longer starts a new one by mistake.
- The usage figure next to the composer works again.

## Knowledge Base

- **The page is simpler.** A health strip at the top counts work cards, what you added by hand, what is indexed and what has been used, with a self-check button. The map is tucked behind **Show map** until you want it.
- **Removing something really removes it.** Deleting a source, a conversation, a dataset or a legal matter now clears its place on the map, its history, and — for files you uploaded — the stored copy of the file. Old leftovers are cleaned up as you go.
- **Market briefings file themselves** like every other result, and sending a result to the Knowledge Base now labels it by what it actually is.
- A web page you upload is read as text, the way a pasted link already was.

## Also in this release

- **A busy model no longer wastes your run.** If the model a job picked is unreachable, DPSBuddy retries once on the next one and tells you it switched.
- **Indonesian identity numbers are protected.** NIK, NPWP, bank account numbers and local phone numbers are masked before anything leaves your computer, alongside the kinds already covered — and in Finance, a column of names or ID numbers is masked while the amounts beside it are left exactly as they are.
- The gateway address is no longer shown in Settings or on the welcome screen; it was already fixed and not editable.
- Dropdowns in Videos, Images and Presentation no longer clip their text, and scrollbars follow the app's own light and dark colours.

## Files to download

- **Windows:** `DPSBuddy-Setup-0.14.27.exe`
- **macOS (preview):** `DPSBuddy-0.14.27-mac-arm64.dmg` for Apple silicon (M1 and later), `DPSBuddy-0.14.27-mac-x64.dmg` for Intel

## Windows

- Run `DPSBuddy-Setup-0.14.27.exe` and follow the installer.
- The installer is not code-signed yet. If SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC", choose **More info → Run anyway**.
- Installing over an existing copy replaces the app and keeps your settings, workspaces and Knowledge Base. Already-installed copies are offered this version in the app.

## macOS (preview)

- Open the `.dmg` and drag **DPSBuddy** to Applications.
- The preview build is not signed by Apple, so macOS will say it cannot verify the developer.
  - macOS 15 and later: double-click the app, dismiss the warning, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
  - macOS 14 and earlier: right-click **DPSBuddy.app**, choose **Open**, then **Open** again.
  - If it still refuses, run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/DPSBuddy.app` in Terminal.
- **Keychain.** When macOS asks, choose **Always Allow**.
- **Closing and quitting.** Closing the window keeps DPSBuddy in the Dock; click the Dock icon to come back. Cmd+Q quits.
- **Video editing needs ffmpeg** (`brew install ffmpeg`). Everything else works without it.
- **Updates on macOS are manual.** Download the new `.dmg` from here and drag it over the old app; your settings and workspaces stay in `~/Library/Application Support/DPSBuddy`.
- Please report anything odd on macOS on the issues page, with your macOS version and chip.
