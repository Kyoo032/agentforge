# Agentforge 0.14.23

## Legal (preview)

- **Review a matter of .docx files.** Create a matter, say which side you act for, upload the drafts, and run. Agentforge classifies each document, compares the counterparty's draft with your prior turn to find unmarked changes, reviews clause by clause against a playbook checklist and the executed documents, and drafts the deliverables.
- **Checked before you see it.** Every quote is verified verbatim against the source, numbers and cross-references are traced in code, and a second pass reads the draft as opposing counsel. Anything that fails is fixed and checked again, up to three rounds. The verification report is part of the result.
- **Deliverables:** an issues memorandum (DOCX with real tables), a redline with tracked changes and margin comments, a deviation report (XLSX), and a red-flags summary. **Send to Knowledge Base** saves the memo; **Open in Documents** continues from it; **Next turn** starts the following round from this one.
- v1 accepts **.docx only**; convert PDFs and other formats first. Live runs need a gateway key; creating a matter, uploading files, and setting document roles work without one.

## Knowledge Base learns from your work

- Every finished Chat turn, document, research dossier, data analysis, finance brief, image, video, and presentation now leaves a short text card in the Knowledge Base on its own. Files stay where they are; the card points at them.
- Chat uses those cards on later questions, but never its own last answer.
- **Knowledge → Sources** shows the cycle (Work → Saved → Indexed → Retrieved) and a count per type. **Send to Knowledge Base** on a result tells you when it is already there.
- Deleting a conversation or a saved result removes its card.

## Videos

- **Example clips.** Six short example videos made from the prompt templates ship with the app. Click one to load its prompt; nothing is downloaded.
- When the video model refuses a job, you now see the real reason and a hint to try later or pick another model, instead of "success".

## Works without internet

- The app opens, saves settings, and stores knowledge with no network at all. Fonts now ship with the app.
- When the gateway cannot be reached, Chat stops within seconds with a plain message instead of waiting minutes.

## Chat

- While a model is being contacted, Chat shows **Thinking…** and the send button reads **Sending…**. Retry details only appear if the model cannot be reached after three tries.

## Everywhere

- Research, Finance, Data, Documents, Images, Videos, and Presentation scroll again when their content is taller than the window.
- The theme switch is an icon in the rail footer, and active items stay readable in dark mode.
- Personal data in Knowledge Base cards is masked, text that tries to steer the model is never stored as a source, and a workspace can only see its own conversations.

## macOS (preview)

- **Pick the file for your Mac:** `Agentforge-0.14.23-mac-arm64.dmg` for Apple silicon (M1 and later), `Agentforge-0.14.23-mac-x64.dmg` for Intel. Drag Agentforge to Applications. A release without `.dmg` files is Windows-only.
- **First launch.** The preview build is not signed by Apple, so macOS will say it cannot verify the developer. On macOS 15 and later: double-click the app, dismiss the warning, then **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. On macOS 14 and earlier: right-click **Agentforge.app**, choose **Open**, then **Open** again. If it still refuses, run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Agentforge.app` in Terminal.
- **Keychain.** When macOS asks, choose **Always Allow**.
- **Video editing needs ffmpeg** (`brew install ffmpeg`). Everything else works without it.
- **Updates on macOS are manual.** Download the new `.dmg` from here and drag it over the old app; your settings and workspaces stay.
- Please report anything odd on macOS on the issues page, with your macOS version and chip.
