# Agentforge 0.14.22

## Research

- **A real dossier, not a list of snippets.** Research now plans several searches, reads the pages behind the results, and builds a cited dossier: sources with verbatim passages, findings that cite them, contradictions, and open questions. Watch each step as it happens, and cancel a run at any time.
- **Results are saved.** Reopen earlier runs from **Reopen saved research…**, or switch between the **Notes** and **Dossier** tabs.
- **Use it elsewhere.** Under each result: **Download Markdown**, **Send to Knowledge Base**, **Make a document**, and **Make a presentation**.

## Data

- **Upload a CSV or XLSX, or paste a table.** Agentforge profiles it, then answers your question by querying it with SQL. Every number comes from a query you can see; evidence tables and charts are built from the query results, not from the model.
- **Ask follow-ups** on the same dataset, and come back to it later from **Saved datasets**.

## Finance

- **Your figures, computed in code.** Paste figures and confirm the line items, type them in, or pick a saved dataset. Margins, growth, runway, breakeven, and NPV / IRR are calculated by Agentforge; the model only writes the narrative.
- **No invented numbers.** Any figure in the brief that does not trace back to your inputs or a computed metric is removed and pointed out.
- **DOCX with real tables** for line items, metrics, and totals, plus an assumptions appendix. Rewrite a single section without regenerating the rest.

## Documents and Presentation

- **Source material.** Paste notes or pick a saved research, data, or finance result, and the draft uses only that material for its facts. A handoff from another mode fills this in for you.

## Everywhere

- Markdown tables now render as real tables in previews and chat answers.
- Web pages fetched for Research or the Knowledge Base are read over HTTPS only, never from local or private addresses.

## macOS (preview)

- **Pick the file for your Mac:** `Agentforge-0.14.22-mac-arm64.dmg` for Apple silicon (M1 and later), `Agentforge-0.14.22-mac-x64.dmg` for Intel. Drag Agentforge to Applications.
- **First launch.** The preview build is not signed by Apple, so macOS will say it cannot verify the developer. In Finder, right-click (or Control-click) **Agentforge.app** and choose **Open**, then **Open** again. You only do this once. If the app still refuses to open, run this in Terminal and try again:

  ```
  xattr -d com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Agentforge.app
  ```

- **Keychain.** Agentforge stores the key that protects your saved settings in the login Keychain. When macOS asks, choose **Always Allow** so you are not asked again.
- **Closing and quitting.** Closing the window keeps Agentforge in the Dock; click the Dock icon to come back. Cmd+Q quits and stops any video work still running.
- **Updates on macOS are manual for now.** The Updates panel will point you here; download the new `.dmg` and drag it over the old app. Your settings and workspaces stay.
