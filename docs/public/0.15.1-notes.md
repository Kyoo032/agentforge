# Nultron 0.15.1

The app on your computer is still named DPSBuddy. These notes are for that installer.

## Updates

- **Check for updates tries again when the connection drops.** A short interruption used to stop the check. The app now tries three times before it shows an error.
- The check talks to the current release page directly, so it no longer depends on the old page name.

## Education

- Education opens with a lesson topic and **Create**. Quiz, pages, the show view, save, and shape tools sit under **More**.
- The lesson uses the same simple slide stage as Presentation.

## Presentation

- Presentation opens with a topic, a length, a style, and **Create**. Once the slides are up, **Download** is the main button.
- The builder, the model, starters, templates, and shape tools sit under **More**.
- **Open Slide** is a second way to build a deck, next to the one you already have. You edit the words on the slide. A selected shape gets a small toolbar.

## Finance

- Finance starts by asking what you want to do, and offers five plain choices.
- You can type the sample numbers as a sentence. The figures are still calculated in the app.

## Chat

- A small figure sits on an empty chat and beside a reply while it is being written.
- A link, a file, or an image in a reply shows as a card.
- Starting a new chat shows the reply immediately.

## First run

- The welcome screen is three steps. A short hello, your Toko Token key, then four examples that open Chat.
- The gateway address is not on that screen.

## Research

- Research no longer asks for a separate search key. It uses Wikipedia and open scholarly sources. A search key you already saved is still used.

## The desk

- Each page scrolls on its own. In Chat and Edit, the box you type in stays put while the thread moves.
- Wide windows use the extra room. Lines of writing stay at a comfortable width.

## Also in this version

- Prompt ideas in the studios sit under the template list.
- Chat uses the width of the desk.
- Video offers only models that answered a live check.

## Files to download

- **Windows.** `DPSBuddy-Setup-0.15.1.exe`
- **macOS (preview).** `DPSBuddy-0.15.1-mac-arm64.dmg` for Apple silicon (M1 and later), `DPSBuddy-0.15.1-mac-x64.dmg` for Intel

## Windows

- Run `DPSBuddy-Setup-0.15.1.exe` and follow the installer.
- The installer is not code-signed yet. If SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC", choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- Installing over an existing copy replaces the app and keeps your settings, workspaces, and Knowledge Base. An installed copy can take this version from inside the app.

## macOS (preview)

- Open the `.dmg` and drag **DPSBuddy** to Applications.
- This preview is not signed by Apple, so macOS will say it cannot verify the developer.
  - macOS 15 and later. Double-click the app, dismiss the warning, then open **System Settings**, **Privacy & Security**, and choose **Open Anyway**.
  - macOS 14 and earlier. Right-click **DPSBuddy.app**, choose **Open**, then **Open** again.
  - If it still refuses, run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/DPSBuddy.app` in Terminal.
- **Keychain.** When macOS asks, choose **Always Allow**.
- **Closing and quitting.** Closing the window keeps DPSBuddy in the Dock. Click the Dock icon to come back. Cmd+Q quits.
- **Video editing needs ffmpeg** (`brew install ffmpeg`). Everything else works without it.
- **Updates on macOS are manual.** Download the new `.dmg` from here and drag it over the old app. Your settings and workspaces stay in `~/Library/Application Support/DPSBuddy`.
- Please report anything odd on macOS on the issues page, with your macOS version and chip.
